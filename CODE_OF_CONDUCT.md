# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this community to be welcoming and free of harassment for everyone. That applies whatever your age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and kind, even when you disagree
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy toward other community members

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code. They may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is harmful.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers through the repository's private [security advisory form](https://github.com/Dev-Xrab/self-host-form-app/security/advisories/new) or by messaging [@Dev-Xrab](https://github.com/Dev-Xrab). All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
